B.TECH CAPSTONE PROJECT

About the Project

A prototype for understanding and estimating campus-related emissions.

01

Problem Statement

Educational campuses generate carbon emissions through electricity consumption, transportation, fuel usage, paper consumption and waste generation. Without a structured mechanism to collect activity data and estimate emissions, it can be difficult to understand the overall campus carbon footprint and identify areas where emissions may be reduced.

02

Proposed Solution

The Campus Carbon Footprint Calculator is a digital prototype designed to collect relevant campus activity data and estimate associated carbon dioxide equivalent emissions. It presents category-wise estimates and an overall estimated campus footprint.

03

Main Objectives

  • Collect relevant campus activity data.
  • Estimate emissions using emission factors.
  • Present category-wise carbon footprint information.
  • Calculate an overall estimated campus footprint.
  • Help identify major emission sources.
04

Project Scope

The current prototype focuses on five measurable categories: electricity, transportation, fuel, paper and waste. Accuracy depends on the quality of activity data and the emission factors selected.
